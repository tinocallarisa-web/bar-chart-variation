BAR CHART WITH VARIATION % — TIPS & HINTS
==========================================


GETTING STARTED
---------------
Minimum setup: drag one field to Axis and one measure to Values.
The variation % between consecutive bars appears automatically. No DAX required.

Add Small Multiples: drag a category field to the Small Multiple bucket to split
the chart into one panel per category, all sharing the same Y scale.


VARIATION %
-----------
. The % is calculated as (current - previous) / |previous| x 100
. The first bar never shows a variation arrow (no previous value to compare)
. Use Variation Labels > Decimal Places to control precision
    0 = whole numbers   |   1 = one decimal   |   2 = two decimals
. Toggle arrows on/off with Show Arrows
. Toggle dashed connector lines with Show Connectors
. Set positive/negative colors independently


COMPARING AGAINST A PLAN [PRO]
------------------------------
By default each bar is compared with the one before it. That answers "is this
month better than the last one". To answer "are we on plan", bind one of the
three reference measures and pick it:

    Format pane > Comparison > Compare to
    Previous bar ... the category before it on the axis (free, the default)
    Previous Year .. your same measure for the prior year or period
    Plan / Budget .. the planned or budgeted figure
    Forecast ....... the forecast figure

. Every bar then gets a horizontal marker at the reference level. The bar is the
  actual figure and the marker says where it should be
. The reference is included in the Y scale, so a plan above actual still fits
  inside the panel instead of being drawn off the top
. Variance > Absolute shows the size of the gap instead of its percentage, or
  Both to show the two
. No DAX needed: bind the measure you already have. The visual does not step
  back through a date table, it reads the measure you gave it


SMALL MULTIPLES
---------------
. Sort Order: sort the axis categories ascending or descending by label
    Auto ......... Power BI default order
    Ascending .... A to Z, smallest to largest
    Descending ... Z to A, largest to smallest
. Columns: set how many panel columns you want; 0 lays them out automatically.
    It is capped at the number of panels — asking for six columns with two
    panels would leave four empty cells and shrink the two that matter
. Min Panel Width: set a minimum px width; the visual scrolls horizontally if needed
. Panel Title > Position: Top or Bottom — use Bottom to save vertical space
. Panel Title > Style: a separator line, or a filled header with its own colour.
    If your title colour would not read against the band, it flips to black or
    white by itself — you cannot configure an invisible title by accident
. Panel Background: add a subtle fill color to each panel to aid separation


SIZE AND LAYOUT
---------------
The layout follows the size of the visual. In a small tile the legend, the Y
axis, the panel title and the X labels step aside, in that order, so the bars
and their percentages stay legible; they come back as you enlarge it.

. Your settings are not changed. What you turn on is the maximum, not an
  obligation: the format pane keeps showing what you chose
. The variation labels are the last thing to go, because they are the point of
  the visual. If they disappear, the visual is too small to be useful there
. If you need an element to stay at a small size, give the visual more room —
  there is no way to force it, because forcing it is what produced a chart with
  ten pixels of bars underneath a stack of labels


ANALYTICAL LINES [PRO]
----------------------
. Average line .... most useful starting point, shows bars above/below the mean
. Max / Min ....... highlights the range of your data
. Median .......... robust central tendency, less sensitive to outliers
. Reference line .. enter any fixed target (budget, KPI, threshold)
. Band ............ define a min/max corridor — bars inside the band are on track
. Lines and bands draw across all panels simultaneously


PER-BAR COLOURS [PRO]
-----------------------------
. Format pane > Bar Colors (Pro) — one colour picker per category
    Example: red if Sales < Budget, green if Margin > 20%
. Per-bar colours override the default bar color but respect the negative color logic


TOOLTIPS
--------
. Default tooltip shows: value, previous, absolute change, and variation %
. Drag extra fields to the Tooltips bucket to add custom rows
    Examples: units sold, region, budget amount
. Tooltip fields inherit the format defined on the measure


INTERACTIVITY
-------------
. Click a bar .............. cross-filter other visuals on the page
. Ctrl+click (Cmd on Mac) .. multi-select bars across panels
. Right-click a bar ........ Power BI context menu (drill, keep/exclude, etc.)
. Drill-down ............... supported when Axis has a hierarchy
    Use the arrow buttons that appear in the top-left of the first panel


PERFORMANCE TIPS
----------------
. Keep Axis values under 50 per panel for best readability
. For large reports, enable Synchronize Slicers in Power BI options
. The visual handles up to 1000 categories and 100 series natively


ACCESSIBILITY
-------------
. The Windows high contrast theme is followed with nothing to turn on: the
    visual takes the theme's own colours, and your colours return untouched
    when you turn the theme off
. Format pane > Accessibility > High contrast draws a diagonal stripe pattern on
    negative bars instead of relying on colour alone. That one is for colour
    blindness and for printing, and it is independent of the Windows theme
. The format pane is available in English and Spanish
. If the report author turns interactions off for the visual, clicking a bar no
    longer filters the page

COMMON QUESTIONS
----------------
Q: Why is the first bar missing a variation arrow?
A: There is no previous value to compare against. This is by design.

Q: Can I show variation against a plan instead of the previous bar?
A: Bind your plan measure to the Plan / Budget well and set Comparison >
   Compare to > Plan. Every bar gets a marker at the plan level. Previous Year
   and Forecast work the same way. For a single fixed number that is the same
   for every bar, use an Analytical Lines reference line instead.

Q: Do I need a date table or DAX for the Previous Year comparison?
A: No. The visual does not step back through a calendar — it reads the measure
   you put in the well. If you already have a "Sales LY" measure, bind it.

Q: My panel title disappeared when I made the visual smaller.
A: That is deliberate. Below a certain size the ornaments are removed so the
   bars stay legible. Your setting is untouched and the title returns when you
   enlarge the visual.

Q: The panels look too small. What can I do?
A: Increase Min Panel Width in Panel Layout. The visual will scroll horizontally.

Q: Why are my per-bar colours not showing?
A: Per-bar colours require a Pro licence. They are also stood down while the
   Windows high contrast theme is active, because a colour picked from a swatch
   has no contrast guarantee against that theme.

Q: Can I use this visual in Power BI Service / Embedded?
A: Yes — it works in all Power BI environments that support custom visuals.
