BAR CHART WITH VARIATION % — TIPS & HINTS
==========================================


GETTING STARTED
---------------
Minimum setup: drag one field to Axis and one measure to Values.
The variation % between consecutive bars appears automatically. No DAX required.

Add Small Multiples: drag a category field to the Small Multiple bucket to split
the chart into one panel per category, all sharing the same Y scale.


VARIATION %
-----------
. The % is calculated as (current - previous) / |previous| x 100
. The first bar never shows a variation arrow (no previous value to compare)
. Use Variation Labels > Decimal Places to control precision
    0 = whole numbers   |   1 = one decimal   |   2 = two decimals
. Toggle arrows on/off with Show Arrows
. Toggle dashed connector lines with Show Connectors
. Set positive/negative colors independently


SMALL MULTIPLES
---------------
. Sort Order [PRO]: sort panels alphabetically or by value
    Auto ......... Power BI default order
    Ascending .... A to Z, smallest to largest
    Descending ... Z to A, largest to smallest
. Min Panel Width: set a minimum px width; the visual scrolls horizontally if needed
. Panel Title > Position: Top or Bottom — use Bottom to save vertical space
. Panel Background: add a subtle fill color to each panel to aid separation


ANALYTICAL LINES [PRO]
----------------------
. Average line .... most useful starting point, shows bars above/below the mean
. Max / Min ....... highlights the range of your data
. Median .......... robust central tendency, less sensitive to outliers
. Reference line .. enter any fixed target (budget, KPI, threshold)
. Band ............ define a min/max corridor — bars inside the band are on track
. Lines and bands draw across all panels simultaneously


CONDITIONAL FORMATTING [PRO]
-----------------------------
. Format pane > Bar Colors — each category shows a color picker + fx button
. Click fx to set rules based on any measure field
    Example: red if Sales < Budget, green if Margin > 20%
. CF colors override the default bar color but respect the negative color logic


TOOLTIPS
--------
. Default tooltip shows: value, previous, absolute change, and variation %
. Drag extra fields to the Tooltips bucket to add custom rows
    Examples: units sold, region, budget amount
. Tooltip fields inherit the format defined on the measure


INTERACTIVITY
-------------
. Click a bar .............. cross-filter other visuals on the page
. Ctrl+click (Cmd on Mac) .. multi-select bars across panels
. Right-click a bar ........ Power BI context menu (drill, keep/exclude, etc.)
. Drill-down ............... supported when Axis has a hierarchy
    Use the arrow buttons that appear in the top-left of the first panel


PERFORMANCE TIPS
----------------
. Keep Axis values under 50 per panel for best readability
. For large reports, enable Synchronize Slicers in Power BI options
. The visual handles up to 1000 categories and 100 series natively


ACCESSIBILITY
-------------
. Enable High Contrast Mode in Format pane > Accessibility
    Negative bars render with a diagonal stripe pattern instead of color alone
    Useful for color-blind users or printed reports

COMMON QUESTIONS
----------------
Q: Why is the first bar missing a variation arrow?
A: There is no previous value to compare against. This is by design.

Q: Can I show variation against a fixed base instead of the previous bar?
A: Use a Reference Line set to your base value.

Q: The panels look too small. What can I do?
A: Increase Min Panel Width in Panel Layout. The visual will scroll horizontally.

Q: Why are my conditional formatting colors not showing?
A: Conditional Formatting requires a Pro license.

Q: Can I use this visual in Power BI Service / Embedded?
A: Yes — it works in all Power BI environments that support custom visuals.
