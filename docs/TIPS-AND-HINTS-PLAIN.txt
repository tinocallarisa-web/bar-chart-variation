BAR CHART WITH VARIATION % — TIPS & HINTS
=========================================

GETTING STARTED
---------------

Minimum setup: drag a date or category field to Axis and a measure to Values. The
variation % between consecutive bars appears by itself — no DAX required.

Setup guide: while you edit, a small panel in the corner lists the optional fields and
then the style templates. Close it and it stays closed; turn it back on under Style
template → Setup guide.

PLAN VS ACTUAL (PRO)
--------------------

- Drag your plan to Plan / Budget (or last year to Previous Year, or a Forecast) and set
  Comparison → Compare to.
- Each bar is the actual figure; a marker shows the reference; the label is the variance
  against it.
- A bar below its reference takes the negative colour, even if it grew against the month
  before.
- Variance format: Relative (%), Absolute (in the units of your measure) or Both.
- Show connectors joins each label to its bar, through the marker.

VARIATION AGAINST THE PREVIOUS BAR
----------------------------------

- The % is (current − previous) / |previous| × 100, between each bar and the one to its
  left.
- The first bar has no label: there is nothing before it.
- Sorting by value changes the neighbours, so the variation follows the new order.
- Decimal places, arrows and connectors are under Variation labels.

ORDER AND EMPTY MONTHS
----------------------

- Axis → Sort order → Auto puts date and number axes in time order. For month names, use
  Sort by column (month number) in the model.
- Future months with no data show as −100%: turn on Axis → Hide empty categories.

SMALL MULTIPLES
---------------

- Drag a field to Small Multiple: one panel per value, all on the same scale.
- Panel layout → Columns: 0 is automatic. Min panel width / height with Enable scroll
  keep panels readable when there are many.
- Panel title: top or bottom, a line or a filled header.
- Free draws 3 panels; Pro up to 100.

LOOK
----

- Colours come from the report theme until you choose your own.
- Style template: Clean (default), Classic, Minimal, Contrast, Compact, IBCS style. A
  template only fills what you have not set.
- Panel layout → Orientation → Horizontal for long category names.
- On narrow bars the variation labels drop the arrows, shrink a little and alternate
  heights rather than overlap.
- Format on the canvas: in format mode, click a bar, a label or an axis.

ANALYTICAL LINES (PRO)
----------------------

- Average, maximum, minimum, median and a reference value, as solid, dashed or dotted
  lines with labels.
- Trend line: a least-squares line per panel. Use it on a time axis.
- Band: with min and max at 0 it marks the typical range (25th to 75th percentile); type
  limits for your own range.

COLOURS WITH RULES
------------------

- Bar settings → Default bar color → fx: colour each bar by a rule, a field or a
  gradient (Pro).
- Power BI applies rules per bar only without a Small Multiple field, as with native
  charts that have a Legend.
- Reset to default on a card clears its settings, including an old rule.

INTERACTION
-----------

- Click a bar to filter the page; click it again or the background to clear. Ctrl/Cmd +
  click adds.
- Bookmarks keep the selection. Right-click opens the context menu. A date hierarchy
  drills from the visual header.
- Keyboard: Tab between bars, Enter or Space selects, Esc clears.
