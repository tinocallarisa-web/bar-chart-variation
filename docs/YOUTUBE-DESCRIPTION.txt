YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION — FULL VIDEO
==========================================

A bar chart that compares each month with the one before answers "is this month better than the last one". It does not answer the question the meeting is about: are we on plan. Bar Chart with Variation % writes the variance on every bar by itself and shows your plan, budget, forecast or last year — as a marker where each bar should have been, or as a bar beside the actual. No DATEADD, no variance column.
