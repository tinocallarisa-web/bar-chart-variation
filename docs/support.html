How to Set Up the Visual

With no fields yet, the visual shows a short getting-started page.

After adding the visual to a report page, drag fields into these data roles:

Data RoleDescriptionRequired
AxisCategory values on the X axis (e.g. months, products, countries). Up to 1,000 categories.Yes
ValuesThe numeric measure. Variation % between consecutive bars is calculated automatically.Yes
Small MultipleSplits the chart into one panel per value, all on a shared Y scale. Up to 100 panels.No
TooltipsExtra measures shown when hovering a bar.No

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I only see 3 panels?
Where licences cannot be checked (Publish to Web, embedding, exports) the visual shows the first 3 Small Multiple panels and a note saying how many there are. While editing a report in Power BI Desktop or the Service without a licence, all panels are shown as a Pro preview with a watermark; in reading view the first 3 are shown. A Pro licence shows up to 100 panels with no watermark.
How does the variation % calculation work?
Between each pair of consecutive bars: ((B − A) / |A|) × 100.
The panels are too small and there is no scroll bar.
In Panel Layout, keep Enable Scroll on and raise Min Panel Width or Min Panel Height. When the panels no longer fit, the visual scrolls horizontally, vertically, or both.
How do I change colours?
Series Colors sets one colour per Small Multiple panel. Bar Colors (Pro) sets one colour per category. The default and negative bar colours are under Bar Settings, and the legend text colour under Legend.
How do I activate my Pro license?
Purchase the Pro plan on Microsoft AppSource. Once your subscription is active, the visual detects it automatically the next time you open or refresh your report — no additional steps required. Microsoft notes it can take up to an hour for a new licence to be recognised: refresh the browser (F5) or close and reopen Power BI Desktop. In Publish to Web and exports Power BI cannot check licences, so the free experience is shown there.
The visual is not rendering. What should I check?
Make sure at least the Axis and Values fields are populated. If the issue persists, contact us at support@tcviz.com.
